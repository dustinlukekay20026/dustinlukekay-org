Helping a family after a house fire

In January 2023, I helped organize immediate support for a North Texas family displaced by a house fire. That meant helping arrange a temporary hotel stay, providing food, and bringing in other people who could help. The first priority was getting the family a place to stay and something to eat while they dealt with what came next.

KXII News coverage from Denison

Lauren Rangel reported on January 25, 2023, that neighbors rescued my former classmate’s son and that the GoFundMe I launched raised more than $2,000. The story shows how people helped the family with immediate needs after the fire.

Preparing meals and supplies for neighbors

My family and I have prepared meals and care packages for people experiencing homelessness in North Texas. Our outreach has included clothing, everyday essentials, and supplies for people caring for pets.

Our family outreach has included shopping for clothing and supplies, gathering essentials, and sharing prepared meals. The photos below show that work, from a sleeping bag ready to hand out to the meals and care packages we put together.

Supporting military service members as an employer

I served in the U.S. Navy from 1996 to 2004. After my military service, I received a U.S. Army Freedom Team Salute commendation for supporting a soldier as an employer.
